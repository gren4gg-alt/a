About

How to play

You wake up in a dark house that rearranges itself every time. Somewhere in it is a green light. Walk into it and you are out.

  • Find the way out. The exit is always the furthest room from where you start. There is no map.
  • Take what you can carry. Glowing objects are worth money, and rarer ones are worth a lot more. You keep nothing if you do not get out.
  • Something is in there with you. It cannot see through walls and it cannot hear you if you stand still. Running makes you loud. It throws knives, and walking in a straight line is how they hit.
  • Getting hit knocks you down, not out. Alone that is the end. With friends, someone has to reach you and hold the pick-up key before you bleed out.
  • The door wants four things. Four objects are scattered through the house, each sitting by a screen you have to beat to release it. Carry them back and set them in the door's four holders. It does not open until all four are in.
  • The screens are loud. Every second one is on, it is calling them towards you. Walk away at any time with Escape or by clicking off it — you lose only your progress on that puzzle.
  • Closets. One per ten rooms, one person each. Inside you are invisible to them and can see out through the peephole. From outside, a lit peephole means it is already taken.
  • Crawl holes are too low to stand in and too low for them to follow. Crouch to get through.
  • You leave together. Everyone still standing must be at the exit, and nobody may be on the floor. Someone has to be revived or written off before the door opens.

Money

Escaping pays a base, plus what you carried out, plus a bonus for every second under par. Par is measured from the actual house you were given, not guessed. Harder houses multiply all of it. Getting knocked out for good pays nothing.

Privacy

This is worth reading properly, because multiplayer means peer to peer here and that has real consequences.

  • No account, no login, no analytics, no cookies. Nothing is tracked and nothing is sent to us, because there is no us — the game is static files running in your browser.
  • Your settings and your bank balance are stored in your browser's local storage on your own machine. Clearing site data erases them. They are never uploaded.
  • Multiplayer is direct between players. A public signalling server is used only to introduce two browsers to each other; after that, position data and voice travel straight from your machine to theirs and never through any server we control.
  • That introduction reveals your IP address to the other players in the room, and theirs to you. This is how WebRTC works and it is unavoidable for peer to peer. Only join rooms whose code you got from someone you are willing to share that with.
  • The microphone is optional. It is only requested when you open or join a room, audio is only captured while your talk key is held if push to talk is on, and nothing is ever recorded or stored. Refuse permission and everything else still works.
  • The room code is not a password. Anyone who has it can join until the room is full.

Credits

Built on three.js for rendering and PeerJS for the peer connections. All sound is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API — there are no audio files. Every house is generated from a seed, so the same seed always produces the same house.
